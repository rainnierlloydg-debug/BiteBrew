ABOUT THE SYSTEM

From the first order
to the daily sales report.

A shared workspace for owners, cashiers and kitchen staff,
with access based on each person's role.

01 / ORDERS

Organized order taking

Record products, options and customer details through the POS. Track orders from Pending through preparation to completion.

02 / KITCHEN

Clear preparation flow

Kitchen staff see the orders they need to prepare and update their progress through the kitchen display.

03 / PAYMENTS & REPORTS

Visible sales records

Record cash and GCash payments, verify collections, and review sales reports with role-based access.

04 / ADMINISTRATION

Manage the business

Owners manage products, prices and staff permissions. Archived accounts lose access while transaction history stays available.
